summary: "The first comprehensive checklist of the Darwin wasps (Ichneumonidae) of Syria: 89 taxa in 54 genera and 15 subfamilies, with illustrated types and six lectotype designations."
blog: "Together with colleagues from Malaysia, Thailand, Colombia, Austria and the United Kingdom, we brought together everything known about the Darwin wasps of Syria. The checklist lists 89 taxa, traces their name-bearing types to 25 institutions worldwide, and illustrates type and comparative specimens. We designate six lectotypes, record three genera and four species from Syria for the first time, and provide DNA barcodes for five species. Comparing Syria with the rest of the Middle East shows a moderate recorded richness, but also how much of the fauna is still waiting to be discovered."
